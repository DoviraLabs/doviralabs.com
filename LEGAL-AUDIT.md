## Website evidence and tracking audit

Inspected the project file inventory, source pages/components/layout/data, entry point, router, package manifest, build configuration, HTML, public assets/manifest/redirects and build script. Searched application/configuration text for cookies, storage, network calls, analytics/telemetry, tracking, SDK names, embeds, external resources, corporate status and contact/legal statements. Package-install tooling and registry references are not website integrations.

Findings in this implementation:

- No analytics, advertising tracker, tracking pixel or third-party tracking script integration.
- No application cookie writes, authentication flow or non-essential-cookie implementation.
- No application localStorage, sessionStorage, IndexedDB or service-worker use.
- No iframe or embedded third-party service; no remote fonts, videos or image requests in website markup/styles.
- No application fetch, XMLHttpRequest, beacon, API/backend or form submission.
- Website assets and React bundle are served locally/same-origin. React/React Router are bundled libraries, not external hosted services.
- Contact links use mailto; opening a link is not a website form submission. Sending email involves email services outside this repository.
- Canonical/Open Graph URLs and SVG namespace URLs are metadata/identifiers, not tracking integrations.
- No US registration, incorporation, registration number, physical address or phone claim found in current website content.

No cookie banner was added. These findings do not prove that the confirmed Cloudflare host, edge configuration, email service or upstream infrastructure has no logs or identifiers. No production infrastructure/account settings were inspected or modified. No broad “we collect no data” claim was added.
